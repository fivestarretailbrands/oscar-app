# Oscar

Oscar is the internal operations software of **Five Star Retail Brands LLC** and its sister company **Ecommerce World
LLC**, which run online stores in the United States. It files our stores' orders into our own task and accounting
systems, including our own QuickBooks Online companies.

Oscar is not offered to anyone else: there is no public sign-up, and it connects only to QuickBooks companies owned by
Five Star Retail Brands LLC and Ecommerce World LLC. It is set up and used by our own staff.
