# Oscar privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Oscar is internal software of Five Star Retail Brands LLC and Ecommerce World LLC ("we"). This policy explains what
Oscar does with data from QuickBooks Online.

**Whose data.** Oscar connects only to our own QuickBooks Online companies. The data it handles is our own business
data: customer records (name, e-mail address, order number) of people who bought from our online stores, and the
payment records that belong to those orders.

**What Oscar does with it.** It creates and looks up customer records for our store orders and links payment records
to the right customer, so our books are complete. It does not use the data for advertising, does not sell it, and does
not share it with anyone outside our companies, except our hosting provider (below) as needed to run the software.

**Where it is kept.** Oscar runs on Google Cloud in the United States. The QuickBooks access tokens are stored
encrypted in Google Cloud Secret Manager and are readable only by our own systems and administrators. Customer data
stays in QuickBooks and in our own systems; Oscar keeps no separate copy beyond short-lived operational logs.

**How long.** Operational logs are kept for a limited period (normally 30 days) and then deleted. When a QuickBooks
company is disconnected, Intuit revokes its access tokens and we remove them from our systems.

**Deletion and questions.** To ask what data we hold about you, or to have it deleted where the law allows,
[contact us](https://www.fivestarretailbrands.com/contact).

**Changes.** We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
