# Connect Oscar to QuickBooks

Oscar is connected to QuickBooks Online only by Five Star Retail Brands staff, for the QuickBooks companies of Five Star
Retail Brands LLC and Ecommerce World LLC. There is no self-service connection for other companies.

If you are on our team and need to connect or reconnect a company, follow the internal runbook or
[contact us](https://www.fivestarretailbrands.com/contact).
